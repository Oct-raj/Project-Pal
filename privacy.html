Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Project Pal. This Privacy Policy explains what information our student collaboration platform may collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Project Pal, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Account and profile information.
  • Projects you create or join.
  • Connection requests and related activity.
  • Messages you send through the platform.
  • Messages submitted through our Contact Us form.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Display student profiles and projects.
  • Support project collaboration and connections.
  • Enable communication between connected users.
  • Respond to questions and technical issues.
  • Maintain and improve the platform.

3. Data Storage

Project Pal uses Supabase services for authentication and database storage. Information is stored and processed through the services used by the platform.

4. Sharing of Information

Profile and project information may be visible to other platform users depending on the features you use. Private messages are intended for their sender and recipient. We do not intentionally publish private messages as public profile content.

5. Data Security

We use available platform security features and database access policies to help protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You should avoid sharing passwords or highly sensitive personal information through the platform. For questions or requests concerning your information, contact us through the Contact Us page.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Project Pal develops. Any updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, visit our Contact Us page.
