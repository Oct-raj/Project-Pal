More Than Just A Project Platform.

Project Pal is a platform where students can discover projects, connect with like-minded people and turn their ideas into reality. Because great things are built together.

Why Project Pal?

Finding the right teammates and starting a project can be difficult. Project Pal makes collaboration easier by bringing students together in one place.

Our Purpose

Project Pal was created with a simple thought: students should not have to build everything alone. Whether you have an idea or want to become part of a team, this platform helps you take the first step.
