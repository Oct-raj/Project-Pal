HELP & SUPPORT

How Can We Help You?

Find answers to common questions about Project Pal.

What is Project Pal?
Project Pal is a student collaboration platform where students can discover projects, connect with teammates and work together on ideas.
How can I create an account?
Visit the Sign Up page, enter your name, email and password, and complete the registration process.
How can I create a project?
Log in to your account, open the Create Project option, fill in the project details and submit the form.
How can I join a project?
Visit Explore Projects, find a project that interests you and send a join request. The project owner can review it.
How can I connect with other students?
Open Find Teammates, discover student profiles and send a connection request. Once accepted, you can communicate through the messaging feature.
Can I send private messages?
Yes. You can chat with students after they accept your connection request.
Can I use Project Pal for free?
Project Pal is designed as a student collaboration platform. You can use its available features without a subscription.
What should I do if I face a problem?
Visit our Contact Us page and send us a message describing your issue.
